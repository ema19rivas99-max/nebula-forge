# Nebula Forge: Cosmic Architect

A merge game about building a galaxy out of almost nothing. Forge celestial
items, combine matching pairs into stronger ones, and arrange them across a hex
grid where every placement changes what your galaxy produces.

- [Support](support.md)
- [Privacy Policy](privacy.md)

Nebula Forge contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party
trackers.
