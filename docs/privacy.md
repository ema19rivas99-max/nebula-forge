# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 August 2026**

Nebula Forge: Cosmic Architect ("the app") is published by Elmer Rivas.

## What we collect

Nothing.

Your game progress — your board, your currencies, your upgrades and your
purchase history — is stored only on your own device. It is not uploaded to us,
and we have no way to read it.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking
software of any kind. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple provides your Game Center nickname and
the scores you submit so they can be shown on public leaderboards. Game Center
is Apple's service and is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Using it is
optional; you can turn it off in your device Settings and the rest of the game
works unchanged.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment
details, card numbers, or billing address. We receive only Apple's confirmation
that a purchase completed, so the app can unlock what you bought.

## Your data and your rights

Because all game data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it.
There is nothing held on a server for us to return, correct, or erase on your
behalf.

## Children

Nebula Forge is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information
from anyone, including children under 13. The only optional data sharing is
through Apple's Game Center, which requires parental approval for child
accounts.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a
new date above.
